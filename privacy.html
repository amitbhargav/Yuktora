Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Privacy Notice

Last Updated: July 2026

Yuktora ("we", "our") collects and processes your personal data in strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).

1. Data We Collect & Specific Purpose

  • Email Address: Collected solely to issue secure Passwordless Magic Link authentication tokens and manage account access.
  • Job Application Data & Resumes: Processed exclusively to compute match metrics, conduct keyword gap analyses, and generate resume optimizations via our AI Match Engine.

2. Data Retention & Erasure

Your data is stored securely using encrypted Supabase cloud instances. We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active. You hold the absolute right to erasure; deleting your account instantly executes a cascade deletion of all user profiles and tracked job data across our servers.

3. Absolute Data Protection & XSS Sanitization

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. All user-supplied text (resumes and job descriptions) is sanitized strictly as raw textual data tokens before processing to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) and accidental data exfiltration.

4. Grievance Redressal Officer

In accordance with the DPDP Act, for any privacy inquiries, consent withdrawals, or grievances, you may contact our designated Grievance Officer at: privacy@yuktora.vercel.app. We pledge to acknowledge and resolve all valid requests within the statutory timeline.
